Your day in South Bali

Coastal highlights,
made for discovery.

Explore South Bali at a relaxed pace. The order and timing of stops can be discussed when you plan your trip. Entrance tickets and water sport activities are not included unless otherwise arranged.

01
Ocean adventure · Nusa Dua

Water Sport at Nusa Dua Beach

Start the day by the water at Nusa Dua. Choose from the beach's available water sport activities and enjoy the clear coastal setting. Activities and their fees can be selected and paid for separately.

02
Culture · Ungasan

Garuda Wisnu Kencana (GWK)

Visit the GWK Cultural Park and see the monumental statue of Vishnu riding Garuda, one of Bali's most recognizable landmarks. Explore the park's open spaces and cultural attractions at your own pace.

03
Beach · South Bali

Pandawa Beach

Take in the dramatic limestone cliffs and bright waters of Pandawa Beach. Enjoy time to relax on the shore and admire the statues set into the cliffside road leading down to the beach.

04
Coastal scenery · Kutuh

Tanah Barak Beach

Follow the striking road cut through the limestone cliffs to Tanah Barak Beach. This scenic approach makes a memorable stop for photos before taking in the coastline below.

05
Temple · Sunset · Uluwatu

Uluwatu Temple at Sunset

End the day at Pura Luhur Uluwatu, perched above the Indian Ocean. Walk along the clifftop and enjoy the sunset views. Please dress respectfully and follow local temple guidance during your visit.
